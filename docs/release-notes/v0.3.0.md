# Sunset Static v0.3.0

Look and sound. The gray boxes are gone. Every enemy, boss, pickup, shot and wall is now pixel art in the ship's palette, over a striped sunset sun, two scrolling skylines and a rushing neon grid that pulses with the music. Your music picks are in: "Drama" on the start screen and "Neon Laser Horizon" in play. New sounds from Kenney. This completes the MVP plan.

## Five minute test

1. Install over v0.2.0. Your saves stay.
2. The start screen plays "Drama". Press Play and the music changes to "Neon Laser Horizon".
3. Fly the Sunset Grid. Drifters are magenta darts. The sun and two skylines slide past at different speeds. The grid pulses with the beat.
4. Destroy a few enemies. Each one bursts into a pixel explosion with flying bits.
5. Fly through the Laser Lanes. Walls are metal panels with cyan edges.
6. Tester tools > Go to: Bass Tower fight, then The Big Disc phase 2. Check the bosses read clearly.
7. Settings > Reduce flashing: bursts and flashes dim.

## New in this version

- Pixel art drawn in code (`tools/make_sprites.py`): drifter, mini drifter, turret, charger, splitter, shell, four pickups, shots, enemy bullets, bursts, sparks, wall panels, the Bass Tower with speakers and core, The Big Disc and its core.
- Three parallax layers: the sky with the striped sun, a far skyline and a near skyline with lit windows. The floor grid rushes at play speed.
- The grid and the pod's ring pulse on every second beat of the play music (pillar 4).
- Hit effects: pixel bursts, flying bits, sparks on blocks and clanks, a muzzle flash, an engine trail and a brighter charge glow.
- Enemy bullets got a dark outline ring, so they read on the sun and the grid.
- Music (G5.21, G8.1): "Drama" by Holizna (CC0) on the start screen. "Neon Laser Horizon" by Kevin MacLeod (CC BY 4.0) in play. Both trimmed to 150 second loops and level-matched (G5.28).
- Sounds: Kenney's Sci-fi Sounds and Digital Audio packs (CC0) for shots, beam, explosions, hits, the shield break, the pod, gates and pickups.
- The Bass Tower now beats in time with the play music: one beat every 0.75 seconds. Its core opens for 2 beats out of 5.

## Checks for the MVP

- Look check (G7.11): the approved ship and pod references sit beside four gameplay screenshots. One mismatch fixed: the pod read gray in game, so it now has the cyan ring from its reference.
- Colorblind check (G5.25): the Bass Tower fight as seen with protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia. Enemy bullets stay round with a dark ring and a white core. Your shots stay long and thin. The open core reads by brightness. Pass.
- Name check (G4.8): a web search on 2026-11-19 found no game called Sunset Static. Near names: Sunset Vector (an FPS), Beyond Sunset (a synthwave FPS) and Tanuki Sunset (a skating game). Trademark records were not searched. This is not legal advice.
