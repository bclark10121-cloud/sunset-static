# Sunset Static v0.9.0

New painted backgrounds. Stage 1, Stage 2 and the start screen now use high definition paintings (your pick: HD paint). The ship, pod and enemies stay pixel art. The floors stay live neon lines, now with glow and a stronger pulse on the beat (your pick).

## Five minute test

1. Install over v0.8.0. Your saves stay.
2. Start screen: a painted synthwave sky with aurora, stars and the striped sun over a small city. The ship sweeps above the title. The title sits clear of the sun.
3. Play Stage 1. The sky and sun hold still. A far skyline and a near skyline with neon rings scroll at two speeds. The floor grid glows and pulses on the beat.
4. Settings > Tester tools > jump to Stage 2. The arcade hall: ceiling lamps, a far row and a near row of cabinets, claw machines and pinball tables. The checkered floor has thin neon seams that pulse on the beat.
5. Watch enemies, shots and coins over the new scenery. Say if anything is hard to read.
6. Turn on Reduce flashing in Settings. The floor pulse gets much softer on the start screen and in play.
7. In the browser, the first tap or key press goes full screen. Turn Full screen off in Settings and reload: it stays off.

## New in this version

- HD painted backgrounds (your pick, 2026-11-22). Made with Google Gemini (Nano Banana Pro) from the game's own prompts, then cut into scrolling layers. Each stage has a still sky, a far row and a near row.
- Each stage dims its layers its own amount so shots and enemies read on top (pillar 1). Stage 2 is the dimmest.
- Upgraded live floors: glow under every grid line, a glowing horizon and about twice the beat lift. Stage 2's checkers get pulsing neon seams.
- New start screen painting. The sun is sized like the 0.8 sun so the title stays clear of it. Mirrored edges fill wide screens.
- Full screen by default in the browser (G5.11). Up to 120 frames a second (G6.20). The APK asks for the vibrate permission.
- A new test checks calm and Reduce flashing on the floors and start screen.

## Judgment calls

1. The stage paintings are side-on rows, not the deep hallway view of the Stage 2 draft. Rows are what let the layers scroll at different speeds.
2. The start screen sun is smaller than in the draft, to keep the title clear of it (your 11-19 ask). The small tag line still touches the top of the sun, as in 0.8.
3. Past the painting's edges on wide screens, the start screen repeats a mirrored strip of sky and city. A stretched edge showed streaks.
4. The two nearest Stage 2 machines lose about 5 units off their bases where the row meets the floor line.
5. The start screen sun glow still pulses with Reduce flashing on. It is slow, about once a second.
6. The web build grew from 45 MB to 56 MB for the paintings.
