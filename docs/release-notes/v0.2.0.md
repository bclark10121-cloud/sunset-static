# Sunset Static v0.2.0

The pod and both bosses. Stage 1 now runs start to finish: pick up the pod, grab its shots, beat the Bass Tower and take down The Big Disc in three phases. Cruise, Standard and Arcade levels and five assists. The ship is smaller: 40 px by default. Still a gray box: shapes, not art.

## Five minute test

1. Install over v0.1.0. Your saves stay. Open slot 1 or start a new one.
2. Fly into the Pod Drop. A spinning pink pod floats in. Touch it. It links on your nose and fires with you.
3. Press B (or K, or the Pod button). The pod shoots ahead and waits. Press B again. It flies back and links on.
4. Press Y (or L, or Flip). The pod moves to your back. Flip it back.
5. Fly through a pod shot pickup: orange S for Spread, green B for Bounce. The pod's shots change.
6. Settings > Tester tools > Go to: Bass Tower fight. Hit the pink core when it opens on the beat. Speakers fire in turn.
7. Tester tools > Go to: The Big Disc phase 2. Shots bounce off the white ring. Aim for its two gaps, or use a full beam.
8. Let the shield break in a boss fight. The phase starts again and the boss keeps its damage.
9. Settings > Difficulty: try Cruise and Arcade.

## New in this version

- The pod: links on the front, launches ahead, hovers and comes back when called. Flip moves it front or back. It blocks small bullets, hurts what it touches (shell shields too) and fires with the ship.
- Pod shots: Spread fans three shots. Bounce sends two angled shots that bounce off floors, ceilings and walls.
- Speed chips: each one adds a speed step. LB and RB (or Q and E) step it down and up.
- Shield cells: each refills one shield bar.
- Bass Tower mid-boss: four speakers fire fans on the beat. The core opens for two beats out of six. Half health adds a ring burst. Speakers break for bonus points.
- The Big Disc boss in three phases: a spiral, then a ring shield with two gaps and aimed fans, then a moving disc with rings and small drifters.
- Soft fail in boss fights: a broken shield restarts the current phase. The boss keeps its damage.
- Levels (G4.12): Cruise (five bars, slow and fewer bullets, more gates), Standard (three bars) and Arcade (two bars, faster bullets, one gate per section).
- Assists: auto-fire, auto-charge, a smaller hitbox, a slow motion button and a pod that blocks every bullet.
- Score: a best score for each level (G4.4). Boss bonuses. Stage clear screen.
- Ship size: 40 px by default, from your "a little smaller" note. Tester tools keep 32, 48 and 64.
- All ten achievements can be earned now.
- Touch buttons for Pod, Flip and Speed show once you have them.
