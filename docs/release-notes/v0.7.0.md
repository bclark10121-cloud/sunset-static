# Sunset Static v0.7.0

New enemies, no bosses. The Bass Tower, The Big Disc and The High Score are gone. Each stage now ends after its last wave section. Four new enemies join the stages, made from your picks with Gemini and Meshy: the Strobe Pair and Ring Carrier in Grid Run, the Prize Claw and Bumper Bot in Insert Coin.

## Five minute test

1. Install over v0.6.0. Your saves stay.
2. Settings > Tester tools > Go to: 4. Strobe Gate. Two crystal drones hang above and below you. A thin flicker warns you, then a cyan beam joins them for one beat. Fly through between beats, or shoot one drone and watch the other flee.
3. Go to: 6. Carrier Run. A slow hauler crosses with three cyan orbs circling it. Shots clank off the hull until you break the orbs (the pod helps). Crack the hull for a shield cell or speed chip.
4. Fly to the end of Stage 1. It clears with no boss. Press fire to go on to Stage 2.
5. Go to Stage 2: 2. Coin Run or 6. High Score. A claw on a cable follows you along the ceiling, drops on the beat, snaps shut and winds back up.
6. Go to Stage 2: 4. Bonus Round. Saucer bumpers bounce off the floor and ceiling. After each bounce they fire a ring of slow bullets on the beat.

## New in this version

- No boss battles (your call). The three bosses, their tester tools and checks are gone. Stage 1 ends after Carrier Run, Stage 2 after High Score. Clearing Stage 1 opens Stage 2.
- Strobe Pair (Stage 1: Strobe Gate, Skyline Tunnel, Carrier Run): twin drones that link a beam every fourth beat (every third on Arcade, sixth on Cruise). Break either one.
- Ring Carrier (Stage 1: Carrier Run): a tough hauler shielded by three orbiting orbs. Drops a shield cell or speed chip.
- Prize Claw (Stage 2: Coin Run, High Score): tracks you along the ceiling and drops on the beat.
- Bumper Bot (Stage 2: Bonus Round, High Score): bounces around and fires rings of bullets on the beat.
- The four new enemies have more health on Arcade and less on Cruise.
- Achievements: Tower Down is now Grid Runner (clear Stage 1). Disc Jockey is now High Scorer (clear Stage 2). Arcade Ace is clear Stage 1 on Arcade. Earned ones stay earned.
- The beat clock now jumps straight to the new track when a stage starts.

## Judgment calls

- The Ring Carrier model was built from its side concept alone. Gemini's front view put side details on the nose.
- The Bumper Bot is the saucer you picked, so it reads as a bouncing saucer.
- The Strobe Pair drones are drawn on their sides so the crystal lenses face each other across the beam.
- The Meshy renders are brightened a little so they read on the night sky. The carrier hull comes out gunmetal, like the ship.
