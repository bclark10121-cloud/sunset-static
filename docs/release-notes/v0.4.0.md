# Sunset Static v0.4.0

Smoother flying and beat sync. The ship eases up to speed and eases to a stop, thumb drags land smoothly, and sprites now move by single screen pixels instead of chunky steps. Waves, enemy shots and both bosses now move on the beat of "Neon Laser Horizon".

## Five minute test

1. Install over v0.3.0. Your saves stay.
2. Fly up and down with the stick, keys or a thumb drag. The ship should glide, start and stop cleanly and tilt without flicker.
3. Watch the background scroll. The sun, skylines and walls should slide smoothly, with no stepping.
4. Listen while you fly the Sunset Grid. Waves arrive on the beat. The grid flashes on the beat, stronger on every second beat.
5. Fly to the Laser Lanes. Turrets fire on the beat.
6. Tester tools > Go to: Bass Tower fight. The speakers fire on every second beat. The Big Disc fires on the beat too.

## New in this version

- Smoother flying: the ship reaches full speed in 0.07 seconds and stops in 0.05 seconds. Before, it jumped to full speed and stopped dead.
- Smoother touch: a thumb drag now lands over a few frames instead of in one jump.
- Smoother drawing: the ship, enemies, shots, the background and the walls move by whole screen pixels (4 per art pixel). Before, they snapped to whole art pixels, which made moving things step.
- Steadier tilt: the ship's tilt frames follow a smoothed speed, so they no longer flicker.
- Beat sync (pillar 4): a beat clock locked to the play music at 160 BPM, measured from the file. It reads the music's playing position and corrects itself, staying within about 15 milliseconds of what you hear in the browser.
  - Waves arrive on the beat.
  - Turrets and shells fire on the beat.
  - Chargers launch on the beat.
  - The Bass Tower steps on every second beat. The Big Disc fires on the beat.
  - The floor grid and the pod's ring pulse on the beat.

## Shared kit

Kit 1.16.0. The game reads the music player inside the kit's `sound.gd` to find the music position. A small kit helper for this is a good fit for the kit later (judgment call, not proposed yet).
