# Sunset Static v0.1.0

The first test build. Stage 1, Grid Run, as a gray box: shapes, not art. Fly, fire, charge the beam and thread the walls. A hit costs shield, never progress. This build is about feel.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK on your phone, or open Play in your browser.
2. The start screen shows the neon sun, the grid and the ship sweeping across. The title reads Sunset Static in Audiowide, clear of the sun. Title music plays.
3. Press Play, then slot 1. Fly with the stick, WASD or a thumb drag. Shots fire on their own on touch. Tap A or J to fire on a controller or keyboard.
4. Hold A (or J, or the Charge button), watch the meter fill at the bottom, then let go. A pink beam fires and pierces the drifters.
5. Fly into a few bullets on purpose. Each hit takes one of the three shield bars and breaks your combo. When all three are gone the ship glitches and rewinds to the last gold beat gate with a full shield. Your score stays.
6. Settings > Tester tools > Ship size: try 32, 48 and 64 px. Tell me which reads best.
7. Tester tools > Go to: jump to Laser Lanes and Skyline Tunnel. Thread the walls. Chargers flash a yellow arrow on the left, then rush in from behind.

## New in this version

- Stage 1 "Grid Run" from data (G4.9): six sections over about five minutes. Sunset Grid, Pod Drop, Laser Lanes, Bass Tower, Skyline Tunnel and The Big Disc. 63 waves, 32 wall pieces, 10 beat gates.
- Auto-scroll at a set pace. Section names show as you enter each one.
- The ship: eight-way flying, rapid fire and the charge beam. Hold to charge, let go to fire. A full beam pierces whole waves and breaks a shell's front shield.
- Five enemy kinds as shapes: drifter (magenta diamond), turret (orange dome on the floor or ceiling), charger (red arrow from behind), splitter (purple circle that splits in two) and shell (gray with a white front shield).
- Soft fail (G4.1): three shield bars on Standard. A broken shield rewinds to the last beat gate. No lives, no game over, score kept.
- Score and combo. A hit breaks the combo.
- Settings: Auto-fire, Charge button Hold or Toggle (G5.23), Reduce flashing, plus the standard six.
- Start screen (G5.21): neon sun, rushing grid and the needle ship as the hero motion. Audiowide title.
- Own icon (G6.10): the ship over the striped sun.
- Tester tools (G5.9): go to each section, god mode, show hitboxes, ship size 32, 48 or 64 px, break the shield, full shield.
- Ten achievements and seven stats on the Records page (G4.10, G4.11). Five unlock in 0.2 with the pod, bosses and Arcade.
- Landscape and portrait (G6.18). Upright, the play field sits at the top like a handheld.

## Not in this build (coming in 0.2)

- Pixel art, parallax, real music and sound come in 0.3. Music and sounds here are stand-ins.
