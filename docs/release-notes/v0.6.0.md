# Sunset Static v0.6.0

Stage 2 is finished. The High Score waits at the end of the arcade: a giant cabinet whose screen plays a different mini game against you in each phase. Stage 2 now has its own music, BC's pick "Bit Quest", and everything in the stage moves to its 100 BPM beat.

## Five minute test

1. Install over v0.5.0. Your saves stay.
2. Settings > Tester tools > Go to Stage 2: The High Score fight. You hear Bit Quest, not the Stage 1 track.
3. Phase 1, Paddle Ball: walls of bullets slide in with one gap. Fly through the gap. A ball bounces off the floor and ceiling.
4. Shoot the screen. The body of the cabinet clanks your shots off. Only the screen takes damage.
5. Go to Stage 2: The High Score phase 2. Snake: a long snake of bullets winds out of the screen, with aimed fans.
6. Go to Stage 2: The High Score phase 3. Block Drop: blocks of four bullets fly at you. Coin bugs climb out with pickups.
7. Beat it. Stage 2 clear shows your score and your best on that level.
8. On a normal run from Stage 1: the music changes when you fly on to Stage 2.

## New in this version

- The High Score, Stage 2's boss. A cabinet with a HI-SCORE marquee. Its screen is the weak spot, and the game on it shows which phase you are in. Three phases at 100, 66 and 33 percent: Paddle Ball, Snake and Block Drop. Every attack fires on the beat. A broken shield restarts the phase and the boss keeps its damage, like the Stage 1 bosses.
- Stage 2 music: "Bit Quest" by Kevin MacLeod (CC BY 4.0), BC's pick from the decision page. The first 150 seconds with a 4 second crossfade loop, at the same loudness as the other tracks.
- Each stage now names its own music, tempo and first beat in its data file. Stage 1 stays at 160 BPM. Stage 2 runs at 100 BPM. Beat counts scale with the tempo, so Blips and the boss keep the same pace in seconds.
- Stage 2 ends when you beat The High Score.
- Tester tools: Go to Stage 2: The High Score fight, phase 2 and phase 3.
- Colorblind check for Stage 2 (`checks/sunset_static_0.6_colorblind.png`).
