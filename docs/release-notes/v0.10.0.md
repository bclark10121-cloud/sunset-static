# Sunset Static v0.10.0

An HD painted ship and pod. Your needle ship and the Halo Core pod are now high definition paintings in the same style as the 0.9 backgrounds (your picks: "HD paint, brighter neon" for the ship and "HD paint" for the pod). Enemies, coins, pickups and shots stay pixel art for now.

## Five minute test

1. Install over v0.9.0. Your saves stay.
2. Start screen: the HD ship sweeps across above the title.
3. Play Stage 1. The ship is sharp at any screen size, with a glowing engine and neon edges. Fly up and down: it still tilts.
4. Pick up the pod. The painted chrome ring with its magenta core spins as before and docks at the nose.
5. Settings > Tester tools > Ship size: try 32 and 64. Both stay sharp.
6. Menu > Shop: equip the Ember, Frost or Gold hull. The tint still works on the painting.
7. Stage 2: check the ship and pod read well over the arcade cabinets.

## New in this version

- HD painted ship B with brighter neon and the HD painted Halo Core pod. Made with Google Gemini (Nano Banana Pro) from your approved designs, then cut into the game's frames by a new tool.

- Hitboxes, ship sizes, the pod's place and every hull look are the same as 0.9. A new check proves the hitboxes did not move.
