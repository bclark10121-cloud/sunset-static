# Sunset Static v0.8.0

Coins and a shop. Every enemy now drops coins, and after each stage a shop sells permanent upgrades for your ship and pod, helpers and new looks. The charge beam is now your pick, the Neon arrow. The beat gate line is gone, and the game fills the whole width of wide screens.

## Five minute test

1. Install over v0.7.0. Your saves stay.
2. Play. Every enemy you destroy bursts into gold coins. Fly over them. The top of the screen counts your coins.
3. Hold A to charge. A magenta chevron grows at the nose and blinks white when full. Let go: an arrowhead flies out with a streak behind it.
4. Beat gates now show as two small gold diamonds at the top and bottom edges, not a line down the screen.
5. Settings > Tester tools > Shop > Give 500 coins. Then Menu > Shop, or clear a stage and press A.
6. In the shop, A buys the row you are on. Try Shield bar: you get one more shield bar right away. B closes the shop. After a stage clear, closing it flies you on.
7. On a wide phone in landscape, the sky, skyline and floor reach both edges of the screen.

## New in this version

- Coins from every enemy, by size: 1 for drifters, minis and Blips, 2 for turrets, chargers and splitters, 3 for shells, Strobe Pairs and claws, 4 for Bumper Bots, 5 for coin bugs and 6 for Ring Carriers. A broken shield never costs coins. A coin is worth 50 points.
- The shop: opens after each stage clear and from the Menu (Shop). Upgrades are permanent and saved in your slot.
  - Ship: Shield bar (3 levels), Quick charge (3), Hot shots (3), Rapid fire (3).
  - Pod: Pod on launch, Spread shot, Bounce shot (own one and equip it as the starting shot), Pod guard (2), Pod spikes (2).
  - Helpers: Coin magnet (3), Head start speed chips (3).
  - Looks: Ember, Frost and Gold hulls, Cyan and Gold engine trails. Press an owned look to equip or take it off.
- New achievement: Window Shopper, buy something in the shop.
- Charge beam: the Neon arrow (your pick). Damage, size and shell breaking are the same.
- Beat gates: small gold diamonds at the screen edges in place of the full-height line.
- Wide screens (G6.21, new standard): on screens wider than 16:9 the scenery fills the full width, with a soft haze at the far sides so enemies fade in. A 1920 x 1080 screen fills edge to edge.
- Tester tools: Shop > Give 500 coins, Open the shop, Reset upgrades (refunds the coins).

## Judgment calls

- Prices: the full shop costs about 3,900 coins, so it fills out over many runs. The cheapest buys are 50 to 80 coins.
- Coin bugs drop 5 coins plus a 35 percent chance of a shield cell or speed chip (before: one coin, a cell or a chip).
- A coin is worth 50 points (before: 250), since there are many more now.
- The shop uses the kit's list page, the same look as Records. One row per upgrade, grouped by name.
- The browser beat check now judges the second-worst of its 12 samples. One sample in a few runs read 0.074 seconds off on the test machine's slow software drawing, then the next was back under 0.02.
- The extra width on wide screens shows scenery, not more play area, so the game plays the same on every screen.
