# Sunset Static v0.5.0

Stage 2, Insert Coin. Beat The Big Disc and fly on into a giant arcade: cabinets tower over a checkered floor, marquees glow and lamps hang from the ceiling. Two new enemies: Blips that march in rows on the beat, and gold coin bugs that drop coins, shield cells and speed chips. The High Score boss and Stage 2's own music come in 0.6.

## Five minute test

1. Install over v0.4.0. Your saves stay.
2. Settings > Tester tools > Go to Stage 2: 1. Attract Mode. You are in the arcade. The floor is a checkerboard that rushes toward you.
3. Watch a Blip formation: it slides in, holds the front and steps up and down on the beat. One Blip fires on the beat.
4. Go to Stage 2: 2. Coin Run. Shoot the gold coin bugs. Most drop a spinning coin worth 250 points. Some drop a shield cell or a speed chip.
5. Go to Stage 2: 3. Cabinet Row. Thread the cabinets on the floor and the marquees hanging from the ceiling.
6. Open the Menu. Stage 1: Grid Run and Stage 2: Insert Coin pick a stage.
7. On a normal run: beat The Big Disc, then press fire on the clear screen. You fly on to Stage 2 with your score.

## New in this version

- Stage 2 "Insert Coin" in six sections: Attract Mode, Coin Run, Cabinet Row, Bonus Round, Joystick Alley and High Score. About 4.5 minutes. 68 waves, 32 wall pieces, 10 beat gates. Content as data in `data/stage2.json` (G4.9).
- Blips: round one-eyed critters in formations of 4 to 9. A formation slides in, holds, marches on every second beat, fires on the beat (every 8 beats on Cruise, 4 on Standard, 3 on Arcade) and drifts away after 12 seconds.
- Coin bugs: gold beetles that weave toward you. They drop a coin (65 percent), a shield cell (20 percent) or a speed chip (15 percent).
- Coins: 250 points each. A new stat counts them on the Records page.
- The arcade look, drawn in code: a ceiling with strip lights and hanging lamps, two rows of cabinets with neon marquees, a perspective checkerboard floor that pulses on the beat and cabinet-panel walls.
- Stage 2 opens when you beat The Big Disc. Fire on the clear screen takes you on with your score. The Menu has Stage 1 and Stage 2 rows. Your slot remembers the stage.
- Tester tools: Go to Stage 2 sections.
- Until the High Score boss arrives in 0.6, Stage 2 ends at its arena with a clear screen.

Version 0.5.0 installs over 0.4.0 and keeps your saves. Old saves start on Stage 1, with Stage 2 locked until you beat The Big Disc.
