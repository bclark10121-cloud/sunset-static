# Credits

Sunset Static is an original game. The stage, enemies, gameplay and start screen are built in code. No personal names here (G1.11).

## Music

- Stand-in title and play loops made for this game by `tools/make_stand_in_audio.py`. Real tracks from Patch.gg come in 0.3.

## Sounds

- Shot, beam, hit, explosion, shield break, gate and clank made for this game by `tools/make_stand_in_audio.py`.
- Talk blip, pick and click from the starter (`tools/make_starter_audio.py`).

## Art

- The player ship and pod are original designs: concept art through Gemini, 3D models through Meshy (Private licence), rendered to sprites by `tools/render_ship_side.py` and `tools/render_pod.py`.

## Fonts

- Audiowide by Astigmatic. SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/FONT-LICENSE-Audiowide.txt`).

## Tools

- Godot Engine 4.7.2. https://godotengine.org (MIT licence)
